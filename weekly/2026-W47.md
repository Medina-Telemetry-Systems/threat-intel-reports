# Weekly intelligence summary: 16–21 November 2026 (week 47)

This edition covers Monday to Saturday; activity from Sunday 22 November will be included next week.

## Bottom line
- **CISA added 9 actively exploited vulnerabilities** to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog. Seven of them
  came with a **three-day** fix deadline for federal agencies, and CISA asked for forensic triage on three (VMware vCenter,
  Cisco Expressway and Apache Roller), meaning organizations should look for compromise, not only patch.
- **Infrastructure and management servers dominate:** VMware vCenter, Puppet Server and Cisco Expressway give an attacker
  control over many other systems at once. Broadcom and Cisco list no workarounds for theirs.
- **Ransomware leak sites posted 343 victims** across 49 groups in our collection. N0n posted the most (92), and
  health care was the most frequently named sector, just ahead of finance.

**Assessment:**
- **Short deadlines and forensic-triage requests signal active, widespread exploitation** (moderate confidence).
- **Management servers are the priority:** a compromised vCenter or Puppet Server lets an attacker reach every system it
  manages. Restrict network access to both until they are patched.
- **Apache Roller:** we published a detection rule on 17 November, three days before CISA added it to the catalog.
- **SAP NetWeaver:** scored only 6.5 Medium, but it is being exploited; this is a case where exploitation should outrank
  the score.
- **AI infrastructure keeps appearing:** LiteLLM is the second actively exploited AI-infrastructure flaw in two weeks,
  after GPUStack in week 46. These systems often hold API keys and reach internal systems, and may be missing from asset
  inventories (moderate confidence).
- **REDCap:** used widely by research institutions and hospitals for research and clinical data, so health care and
  research organizations should treat it as urgent.

## Ransomware leak-site activity
**343 victim postings** from **49 groups** appeared in our daily collection runs of 17–21 November.

- **Countries:** 303 postings name a country. The United States accounts for 169 of them (56%), followed by Canada (18),
  the United Kingdom (12) and Germany (9).
- **Sectors most often named:** health care (64 postings), finance (59), manufacturing (47), retail and hospitality (38),
  and construction and real estate (31). One posting can mention more than one sector.

**Assessment:**
- **Batch uploads drive the weekly totals again.** N0n, Soulgroup and The Gentlemen each posted most of their victims in a
  single day, so the totals reflect when groups publish, not when attacks happened (high confidence).
- **N0n has moved to the top:** 17 postings last week, 92 this week. A group to watch.
- **Health care overtook finance** as the most named sector. With REDCap added to KEV, health care and research
  organizations face pressure from two directions this week (low confidence: sector tags are keyword-based).

**Limits of this data:**
- Postings are the groups' own claims. Some are exaggerated, re-posted or months old.
- Sectors and countries are tagged from keywords in each posting, so they are approximate.
- Last week's summary counted collection runs through 16 November, so this week starts with the 17 November run to avoid
  counting the same postings twice.

  not yet covered.
