# Weekly intelligence summary: 9–15 November 2026 (week 46)

## Bottom line
- **CISA added 12 actively exploited vulnerabilities** to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog. It gave
  federal agencies **three days or less** to fix 8 of them, a sign of active, widespread exploitation. Edge and
  internet-facing products dominate: Barracuda email gateways, Check Point, Cisco BroadWorks, OpenSSH and IBM Aspera Faspex.
- **Ransomware leak sites posted 201 victims** across 32 groups in our collection. US organizations were the most common
  victims, and finance, manufacturing and health care the most frequently named sectors.
- **INC Ransom posted the most victims (53),** mostly in a single batch. It is profiled this week:
  [INC Ransom](../actors/inc-ransom.md).

**Assessment:** the short deadlines suggest CISA judged that these flaws were being exploited at scale. Most affect
devices that sit on the network edge and are often managed outside normal patch cycles. Organizations running Barracuda
email gateways or Check Point should assume they may already have been targeted and look for signs of compromise, not
just patch (moderate confidence).

- **Countries:** 183 postings name a country. The United States accounts for 84 of them (46%), followed by Canada (13),
  Germany (7) and India (6).
- **Sectors most often named:** finance (53 postings), manufacturing (36), health care (33), retail and hospitality (22),
  and construction and real estate (22). One posting can mention more than one sector.

**Assessment:** large single-day batches from INC Ransom, Pixelleaks and The Gentlemen mean the weekly totals reflect when
groups publish, not when attacks happened. They are not a reliable measure of attack volume this week (high confidence).
The continued US weighting matches longer-term reporting on the major groups.

**Limits of this data:**
- Postings are the groups' own claims. Some are exaggerated, re-posted or months old.
- Collection of this data began on 12 November, so the earliest days of the week are missing.
- Sectors and countries are tagged from keywords in each posting, so they are approximate.
